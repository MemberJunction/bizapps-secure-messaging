What the research surfaced

Key takeaways from studying the current tool and competing products.

TitanFile — workspaces & audit

Email-like simplicity, per-client/matter secure workspaces, two-way unlimited file + message exchange, "Secure Submit" links, and a heavy emphasis on timestamped audit trails — proof of delivery and proof of access — as a compliance selling point.

titanfile.com — Secure Portal + "8 Reasons Clients Love TitanFile"

Liscio — tasks & guided e-sign

Mobile-first client messaging, built-in e-signature with a guided signing experience that coaches clients step-by-step, task tracking and follow-ups woven into the conversation, and an AI assistant for firms.

liscio.me + Moxo / SmartVault comparisons

Our differentiators to lean into

Passwordless token + magic-link auth (zero client setup), messages flowing through MJ's AI pipeline with human approval, and a native audit trail via Record Changes. The mockups surface these instead of leaving them in the backend.
